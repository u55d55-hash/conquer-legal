Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Developer: Sat Node · Contact: u55d55@gmail.com

Conquer is a running game: you record runs, and the ground you cover on foot becomes your territory on the map. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how you control it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy

Username, units, time zone, alert and leaderboard preferencesWhen you set themTo run your profile and the game
Precise location (GPS points with time, accuracy, speed and altitude)Only while you are recording a run, and once when you ask the map to centre on you or add a privacy zoneTo measure your run and work out the ground you covered

We do not collect location in the background when you are not recording, and we do not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Health Connect: Conquer only reads the workout data listed above, and only after you allow it in Health Connect; it never writes to Health Connect. Imported workouts are shown only to you, never count toward hexagons, colours, streaks or leaderboards, and are never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can stop at any time by removing Conquer in Health Connect's app permissions; imported workouts are deleted with your account. Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What other runners can see

  • The shared map: unless you switch it off (You → Settings → Show my territory to other runners), other runners can see which hexagons you have claimed, with your username and colour, and how many you have. They never see your routes, run times or live position, and hexagons within 500 m of where any of your runs started or ended are never shown, so places like your home stay out of it. By default they see the hexagons from a run 2 hours after it ends, so nobody can tell where you are right now; you can choose right away or 6 hours in Settings. The map shows which runner currently holds each hexagon (whoever has run it on the most days lately), and when your run takes hexagons another runner held, both of you see that in the app, the other runner only after your chosen delay. Nobody can take your own ground or totals. Switching sharing off hides your hexagons from everyone else; you still see theirs.
  • Leaderboards: unless you switch them off (You → Settings → Show me on leaderboards), other runners see your username, your colour and your totals on the boards — distance on foot, hexagons, new hexagons this week and your daily streak, for the week, all time and the city where you hold the most ground. Never your routes or places.
  • Friends: you get a private invite code. Anyone you give it to can add you as a friend; you then see each other on the Friends leaderboard (the same username, colour and totals as the public leaderboards). Your friends list is visible only to you.
  • Never shown to anyone else: your routes, run times and locations, your step and activity data, workouts you import, and your email.
  • Share cards are yours to share: if you tap SHARE CARD, the app makes an image of your route’s outline (without a map), distance, new hexagons and colour, and hands it to the app you choose. Everything within 300 m of where the run started and finished is left out of the outline. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database, authentication and server functions), hosted in the Seoul region (ap-northeast-2).
  • Google: Maps SDK for Android (shows the map; Google receives your map requests), Sign in with Google (if you use it), Play Integrity (anti-cheat) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (if you enable notifications).

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (You → Settings → Delete my account). This permanently deletes your account, runs, territory and everything linked to it from our servers. Data stored on your phone for that account (runs waiting to upload) is deleted too. You can also email u55d55@gmail.com to ask for deletion or a copy of your data — see Delete your account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your data is restricted by row-level security so each runner can only read their own private data. Run verification happens on our servers, not on your phone.

Children

Conquer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
