In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Conquer and sign in.
  2. Go to You → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

If you can't open the app, email u55d55@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Conquer account”. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, sign-in details, every run (routes, GPS points, step and activity readings), your territory, XP, badges, challenges, standings, privacy zones and notification token. Ground you held is released to the map. Runs waiting to upload on your phone are deleted too.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.
